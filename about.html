OUR STORY

Every rescue begins with hope.

Paws Animal Rescue was established to provide support to abandoned, neglected and abused animals in the Cape Town community.

We believe that every animal deserves to be treated with kindness and given an opportunity to live a safe and fulfilling life.

Our work focuses on rescue, temporary shelter, basic veterinary care, rehabilitation and finding responsible forever homes.

🌿

Our Mission

Our mission is to protect vulnerable animals by providing rescue, care, rehabilitation and adoption services while promoting responsible animal ownership within our community.

🌍

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a caring community where animals are treated responsibly and fewer animals are abandoned, neglected or left without proper care.
