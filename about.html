Our story

Every rescue begins with hope.

Paws Animal Rescue began in 2016 when a group of friends in Diep River started taking in stray dogs from their neighbourhood. Word spread, the garage filled up, and a small shelter was born.

Today we run two branches in Cape Town, with a vet partner, 65 foster homes and more than 180 volunteers. Our focus is still the same: rescue, shelter, vet care, rehabilitation and finding responsible forever homes.

We believe every animal deserves kindness and a safe, happy life, no matter how they started out.

Our mission

To protect vulnerable animals by providing rescue, care, rehabilitation and adoption services, while teaching our community about responsible pet ownership.

🌍

Our vision

A Cape Town where every pet is wanted, sterilised and cared for, and where no animal is left abandoned, neglected or alone.

What we stand for

Our values guide everything we do.

❤️

Compassion

We treat every animal with patience, kindness and respect, especially the frightened ones.

🤝

Responsibility

We sterilise every animal and support adopters for life, because a pet is a lifelong promise.

☀️

Hope

We believe no animal is a lost cause. Time and love can heal almost anything.

🐾

Community

Volunteers, fosters, sponsors and adopters save lives together. Nobody does it alone.

Our journey

Milestones along the way.

From one garage to two branches, Paws has grown thanks to the people of Cape Town. Here are some of the moments we're proudest of.

  1. 2016

    Three friends start rescuing stray dogs from a garage in Diep River.

  2. 2018

    Paws registers as a non-profit and opens the Southern Branch on Main Road.

  3. 2020

    Our partner vet clinic opens on site, so animals are treated the day they arrive.

  4. 2021

    The foster programme launches, and the 500th animal goes home.

  5. 2023

    The Northern Branch opens in Table View to help animals on the West Coast.

  6. 2026

    More than 1 240 animals rehomed, and still counting.
