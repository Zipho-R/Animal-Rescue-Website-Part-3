How can you help?

Choose your way to make a difference.

Every option below can be chosen in the enquiry form. Not sure? Pick “General enquiry” and we'll help you decide.

🏡

Adopt

Give a rescued dog or cat a forever home. Fees from R850, with vet care included.

🛋️

Foster

Care for an animal at home for 2–8 weeks. We cover food and vet bills.

🙋

Volunteer

Walk dogs, cuddle cats or help at events. Free training, flexible shifts.

💛

Sponsor

From R50 a month, you can feed and care for a rescued animal.
