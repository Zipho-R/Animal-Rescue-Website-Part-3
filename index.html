WHO WE ARE

Giving vulnerable animals a second chance.

Paws Animal Rescue is a Cape Town-based non-profit organisation committed to protecting animals that have been abandoned, neglected or abused.

We provide temporary shelter, basic veterinary care, rehabilitation and adoption services while working to find every animal a safe and loving permanent home.

Learn more about Paws →

WHAT WE DO

Care, compassion and second chances.

Our work focuses on giving vulnerable animals the care and support they need to find loving homes.

🐾

Animal Rescue

We respond to animals in need and provide them with a safe environment away from neglect, abuse and abandonment.

❤️

Veterinary Care

Animals receive basic veterinary attention, treatment and care to support their recovery and wellbeing.

🌿

Rehabilitation

We help rescued animals regain confidence and prepare them for life in a loving permanent home.

🏡

Adoption

We carefully match animals with responsible families who can provide safe and loving forever homes.
