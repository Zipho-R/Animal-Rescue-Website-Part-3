Who we are

Giving Cape Town's animals a second chance.

Paws Animal Rescue is a Cape Town non-profit run by a small team and a big family of volunteers. We take in animals that have been abandoned, neglected or abused and give them shelter, vet care and time to heal.

When they're ready, we carefully match each one with a family that suits their personality, and we stay in touch long after they go home.

  • Two branches: Diep River and Table View
  • Full vet check before every adoption
  • Free advice for adopters, for life

How adoption works

Four easy steps to bring them home.

  1. Choose a friend

    Browse the animals above and read their profiles to find a good match.

  2. Send an enquiry

    Fill in our short online form. We reply within two working days.

  3. Meet and greet

    Visit a branch to spend time together, with the whole family.

  4. Go home together

    After a quick home check, your new best friend moves in.
