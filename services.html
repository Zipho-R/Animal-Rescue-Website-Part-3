WHAT WE DO

Caring for animals in need.

From rescue and rehabilitation to adoption, we work to give animals the care they deserve.

OUR SERVICES

Helping animals heal and thrive.

Paws Animal Rescue provides several services to support animals throughout their journey from rescue to their forever home.

01

Animal Rescue

We help animals that have been abandoned, neglected or abused by providing immediate safety and temporary shelter.

02

Veterinary Care

Rescued animals receive basic veterinary attention and treatment to support their health and recovery.

03

Rehabilitation

We provide a caring environment where animals can regain confidence and learn to trust people again.

04

Adoption

We help match rescued animals with responsible adopters who can provide loving and permanent homes.

05

Foster Care

Foster families provide temporary homes where animals can feel safe while they wait for permanent adoption.

06

Community Education

We encourage responsible animal ownership and help our community understand the importance of animal welfare.
