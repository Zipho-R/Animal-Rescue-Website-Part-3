Our services

Helping animals heal and thrive.

Six services support every animal on its journey from rescue to a forever home.

Animal rescue

We answer calls about abandoned, injured and neglected animals and bring them to safety, shelter and a warm meal.

  • Rescue line 7 days a week
  • Safe shelter

Veterinary care

Our partner vets check, vaccinate, sterilise and treat every animal before it is put up for adoption.

  • Vaccinations
  • Sterilisation
  • Microchips

Rehabilitation

Injured or nervous animals get time, gentle training and lots of kindness until they are confident again.

  • Recovery care
  • Behaviour support

Adoption

We match animals with families based on lifestyle, home and personality, so every adoption lasts.

  • Meet and greet
  • Home check
  • Lifetime support

Foster care

Foster families give animals a calm, temporary home while they recover or wait for adoption.

  • Food supplied
  • Vet costs covered

Community education

We visit schools and communities to teach responsible pet care, sterilisation and kindness to animals.

  • School talks
  • Free workshops
